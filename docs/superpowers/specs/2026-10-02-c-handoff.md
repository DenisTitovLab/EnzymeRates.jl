## What B shipped, and where

Rule 1, flux. Every steady-state kinetic group of an emitted mechanism carries net flux,
decided on the rapid-equilibrium segment graph: `_flux_carrying_steps(groups, rxn)`,
`_flux_carrying_groups(groups, rxn)` and `_flux_carrying_groups(m)`, with `_all_steady_state`
and `_block_balanced`, in `src/mechanism_enumeration.jl`. The flip pre-filters its units on
the all-steady-state graph and treats a flip set whose flipped group carries no flux as failed
(the Apriori search extends it). The split rebuilds a zero-flux part at rapid equilibrium
(`_revert_zero_flux_parts`; `_partition_independent_count`'s counter takes per-step kinds via
`_count_kind`). The test reads each step's metabolite lists, so it holds for fused and
Theorell–Chance steps; a Theorell–Chance parent gets its flip children today.

Rule 2, copies. A copy group (bindings of a `CompetitiveInhibitor`, including a substrate or
product declared as one) is rejected only when it is provably redundant: every complex has a
proportional productive twin (`_productive_twin`: same rapid-equilibrium segment, same
offsets, judged in every conformation where the copy binds, free enzyme always present) AND
the dwell gauge of track 2's Theorem 2 is consistent (`_gauge_rescaling`: one factor on all
of the copy's complexes, its own group checked, `:EqualAI` constants tied across states).
`_twin_only` is the first half, `_redundant_copy_groups(m)` the mechanism-level list.
Enforced in the dead-end placement (on the candidate, before the child is built), the split's
part filter, the `_expand_change_allo_state` filter, and `_assert_emission_rules`, which
`expand_mechanisms` runs on every parent next to `_assert_chemistry_is_iso`. Composition no
longer makes a twin: a productive complex formed by a steady-state binding sits in another
segment and is not proportional. Competition in the dead-end move is decided per site
(`_forms_with_binding_step_native` takes a role; typed per-form bound sets): a copy occupies its
own site, so a form carrying only A\* is a legitimate site for an inhibitor competing with A,
and copies do not count toward the all-substrates/all-products capacity test.
`seed_mechanisms` errors, naming the regulators, when no seed binds every required one.

Soundness evidence (in the record): of every rejection sampled or ranked (200 sampled plus
all 140 the last fix changed), each has fitted − rank ≥ 1 with the copy adding the phantom;
of 100 kept mechanisms the old composition rule would have rejected, 89 are full rank.
Pins in `test/test_mechanism_enumeration.jl`: bi-bi (A[CX] + B[N] ⇌ P[C] + Q[NX]) levels
62 / 369 / 1,200 and, with A, B, P, Q also inhibitors, 62 / 1,649 / 31,730. Enumeration of
the latter to depth 2 is 18% slower than before B (design limit 20%), 6% per mechanism.

## Invariants C must keep

- Every child C's moves emit must pass `_assert_emission_rules`; otherwise the beam records a
  failure row at that child's expansion. Either guarantee it by construction or filter children
  with `_flux_carrying_groups` and `_redundant_copy_groups`. Track 1 measured that a single-step
  MERGE of a steady-state isomerization can leave a group without flux (16 of 3,818 valid
  merges, chemistry groups with an unmerged dead-end mirror); merging whole groups lost none.
- The rank oracle `_testhelper_identifiable_rank` (finite-difference SVD of ∂v/∂log θ, about
  half a second per mechanism) lives at the top of `test/test_mechanism_enumeration.jl`; every
  rewritten exact-children expectation in B was cross-checked with it. Use it the same way.
- Chemistry is still detected by `is_iso` in `_hyperbolic_catalysis`, `_expand_to_allosteric`,
  `_expand_change_allo_state`, `_partial_onlya_catalysis` and the `expand_mechanisms` guard
  `_assert_chemistry_is_iso`; the flux predicate no longer needs it. C replaces that detection
  and the guard when fused and merged parents enter `expand_mechanisms` (findings plan, C).
- The twin test needs no special case for a merged complex: it compares weights (segment and
  offsets), not compositions, so "a merged form carries two compositions" (track 2's open
  question 3) is moot.
- The Canonical Step Form rules of CLAUDE.md still bind: step and group order decide Haldane
  pivots and fitted names; `unique!` works by `==`/`hash` only because construction is canonical.
  A's regression method (keyed by steps with explicit lists; detached worktree for the old
