- Re-ran every P5a script, from copies in my own directory.

## New failure cases

All are in `logs/skp_adv*.log` and `logs/skp_adv2.log`; mechanisms are in `data/adv_cases*.jls`.

1. **AD2e: two free forms; every step of E is `:OnlyA`, F keeps its steps.**
   - The guard admits it, and G1–G3 all pass under both (i) and (iu).
   - Limit 2.8238e-4 vs the package's 2.7861e-4: Δ 1.3e-2 at n = 2, Δ 5.5e-2 at n = 1.
   - Cause: the package flips at E in the active state but at F in the inactive one. F is the first free form of the pruned inactive graph. The package matches deletion-with-flip-at-F to 1e-16.
   - The limit equals deletion when the flip stays at E: Δ 6e-41.
2. **AD2c: package bug.** A second free form F is joined to E only by an `:OnlyA` isomerization, and an inhibitor binds F.
   - The guard admits it.
   - All three inactive-state polynomials are 0; the generated equation contains `0 ^ 2 * …`; the rate is NaN in 4 of 4 samples.
   - The probe's `study` crashes with SingularException.
   - Its V4 oracle silently returns 1.4e-310 instead of failing.
   - Under (iu), G3 rejects it.
3. **AD2b:** admitted today, with a finite deletion rate of 1.5e-5. The limit sequesters the inactive enzyme into free F and gives v → 0, so G2 rejects it. The probe's V4 oracle throws SingularException(4).
4. **AD1 (SS, RE, n = 1) and AD11:** mixed `:OnlyA` groups whose plain member lies on an `:EqualAI` binding square.
   - Admitted today; (i) unique = deletion (AD11: ≠ deletion, Δ 6.9e-4).
   - (iu) cone empty, proven: the nullspace basis `[1,1,0,0]` forces the plain kon exponent to 0.
5. **AD14 and AD14b:** a two-member `:OnlyA` chemistry group whose members need different ratio exponents. Admitted today; (i) cone provably empty (`[1,1,1,1,0]`); (iu) unique = deletion.
6. **AD15:** a split `:OnlyA` binding group. Rejected today; (iu) unique = deletion.

The other 25 adversarial mechanisms hold the probe's claims:
- AD2a and AD2d (two free forms, still connected);
- AD3a–d (inhibitors and `::Inh` copy squares);
- AD4 and AD4b (two-product step);
- AD5 (conformation-changing group);
- AD7a–c (thermodynamically pinned constant);
- AD8 (Theorell–Chance);
- AD9 (RE chemistry inside a segment with steady-state steps);

They give (i) and (iu) unique = deletion. (ii) is non-unique for the steady-state bindings, as predicted. For AD3c (a Wegscheider-only violation) the guard and every cone agree on rejection.

## Verdict

**PARTIAL**, the same as the probe's own verdict, but with two more corrections the spec needs.

The design can rely on:
- the oracle;
- the leading-face engine;
- the cone semantics and conventions (i) vs (ii);
- every reported count (deterministic, reproduced exactly).

It cannot rely on:
- "G1 = today's Stiemke test";
- "deletion + pruning" as the package defines it when there are two free forms;
- the "16 + 3" size of the rejected set;
- the production line estimate.

## Design implications

1. **G1** must be exact cone feasibility for the chosen convention, with per-step classes and untied groups (a Fourier–Motzkin test on the restricted system). It is not today's guard, and the admitted set changes in both directions.
2. **One named flip form**, with pruning seeded only from it. Otherwise reject allosteric mechanisms with more than one form that holds no metabolite and no residual.
3. **State the ping-pong consequence plainly.** Under the limit semantics, essentially every enumerated `:OnlyA` ping-pong mechanism is rejected (80 of 81 tested).
4. **G3 needs either an exact polyhedral uniqueness test or an explicit sampling policy.** Budget for it.

## Open questions for Denis

1. **Two free forms:** reject them in allosteric mechanisms, add a declared flip form, or let every free form flip (a new model)?
2. **`:OnlyA` ping-pong:** reject the whole family, or keep deletion by definition for islands linked only by `:OnlyA` chemistry?
3. **Mixed `:OnlyA` groups** (binding plus chemistry members): per-step classes (iu, which rejects AD1 and AD11), group-as-chemistry (i), or forbid them?

## Surprises and failures

- Parallel steps (AD6) cannot be built: the package refuses them ("a reaction belongs to one kinetic group").
- AD13 found no root-vs-first-seen mismatch in any of 5 conformation names.
- The probe's oracle can return a tiny number instead of failing on a disconnected network.
- The probe quotes 1e-11 to 1e-13 for the three-stage kcat check; the logs show up to 6.4e-11.
- No Julia process of mine is left running.
