## 1. Verdict per probe

**Foundation: PASS. Trust is high for plain mechanisms.**
- It delivered 2,799 deterministic mechanisms and recorded every stage error.
- The oracle meets both criteria: detailed balance ≤ 7.2e-108, and Full-mode agreement ≤ 7.0e-15 wherever Full mode is well defined. Open RE cycles explain all 17 disagreements exactly.
- Every later probe and all three skeptics built on it without finding an oracle error.
- **Weak spot: coverage is thin.**
  - expand_bibi has 700 mechanisms against a 2,000 cap; init_uniuni has 1, init_unibi 7, allo_uniuni 35.
  - There are no ter-ter mechanisms, and inhibitor copies appear only in specs and handwritten.
- **Weak spot: the identifiability baseline understates the problem.** It names one over-count. Skeptic P1 found 24 plain mechanisms whose identifiable rank is below their fitted count, losing 36 dimensions in all.

**P1 engine: PARTIAL. Trust the engine; do not trust its structural predicates.**
- The engine is exact: 806/806 polynomials are identical to the package's, with oracle error 5.8e-35.

**P2 joint solve: claimed PASS, PARTIAL after the skeptic. Trust the core; do not trust the phantom claims.**

## 2. Contradictions between probes

1. **Exponents.** P2 ("every exponent ±1") and P5b ("every exponent an integer") are contradicted by Skeptics P1 and P2: A10g, `half_exponent` and `half_keq` need fractional exponents, and the package itself emits `Keq^-0.5`. Every downstream prototype assumes integers: P1's and P4's `Poly`, P7's IR and P8's key.
2. **Phantom predicates.**
   - P1 calls its speed-free predicate exact; Skeptic P1 finds 11 false negatives and proposes a block-level rule that is "exact in every case I tried".
   - Skeptic P2's F4 cases break that rule too (`tc_pair_iso`, `symmetric_routes_pinned`, fuzz/311). There, kinetic-group ties plus a constant pinned by thermodynamics zero the flux of a step inside a block that does turn over the reaction.
3. **Which free form carries L.**
   - P1 says dfree matches the package 806/806, so the allosteric normalization can be built on it. Skeptic P1 shows dfree is the segment root's weight, not the free form's (A18, A18b, D1).
   - P5b settles the same-segment case: it only rescales L. It then extends the rule to any metabolite-free path, which my Cleland-iso run refutes.
   - Skeptic P5a's AD2e shows the package flipping at E in the active state and at F in the inactive one.
4. **The `:OnlyA` guard.** P5b keeps today's guard "unchanged", and P5a equates G1 with today's Stiemke test. Skeptic P5a shows the guard errs both ways for mixed groups (AD1, AD11, AD14, AD15).
5. **Group alignment.** P2 aligns group members by their conformational change. The package and P6's ported `orient` align chemistry by Tier 1. These give different models (F1).
6. **Sig encoding.** P6 encodes the type parameter as nested heterogeneous tuples decoded by `collect`. P7 warns never to do that: the generator then recompiles per law, 0.25–0.66 s each. Commit 9fca159 already had to bound the same widening in today's `_sig_of` (966 method instances for 239 seeds).
7. **Block factors.** Denis's decision keeps "block / RE-segment factors as products". P1 found blocks save 2.6% of terms after renaming, and P7 found the block-factored variant (facth) emits the same code as the variant without blocks (segh).
8. **Display normalization.** P4's Rule A divides the 49 laws without a constant term by ΠS, so the strings print `1/A` and `P*Q/(A*B)`. Today's strings are division-free in concentrations. P8 found the constant term is a single monomial in only 505 of 743 enumerated plain laws, so "one rule everywhere" means defined everywhere, not clean everywhere.
9. **kcat.**
   - Denis's decision computes kcat with the `:OnlyA` limit engine.
   - P5a's limit-engine kcat is 0 in 42 mechanisms where a dead-end complex captures the enzyme at saturation, and ∞ in 4 that never saturate. `rescale_parameter_values` cannot use either value; 48 of 519 mechanisms are affected.
   - The ROADMAP's planned assay-concentration anchor needs no engine.
10. **Names in the priority.** P2 breaks priority ties by content names, which carry metabolite and conformation names. A name decides the Haldane pick in 34 enumerated mechanisms, and per P8 it breaks the A↔B symmetry of the Theorell–Chance twins. That is independent of rendered names, but not of naming in general.
11. **Line counts.**
    - The brief's "~1,950" is physical lines (1,970); the probes count code lines (920 today).
    - The estimates count some shared pieces twice: the (1 − Γ/Keq) division (P1 21, P4 20, P7 30 lines), the cycle basis (P2 and P6) and the MWC assembly (P5b 50, P7 45).
12. **Minor baseline mismatch.** The Foundation lists 4 opposite-direction folds (allo_bibi/1197, 1212, 1378, 1413); P5b counts 9. Both agree on 30 folds made in one state only, and on 0 after the redesign.

## 3. Claims resting on too little evidence

- **Rational exponents** never passed through the engine, the Haldane division, the IR, the printer or the key.
- **No end-to-end chain exists.**
  - P7 timed laws built with the package's dependents, and checked P2-solved laws for operation counts only.
  - P8 keyed a frozen snapshot of P5b's code.
  - P5a's limits never met P5b's fold table.
  - So the summed size estimates come from prototypes that were never joined.
- **`:OnlyA` under the limit semantics:**
  - The fitted parameter list (R6) was never computed.
  - An exact G1 on per-step classes and an exact G3 were never built.
  - Uniqueness is sampled, though it survived a second draw.
- **The L-carrier rule** rests on 3 synthetic mechanisms; the fourth, mine, breaks it.
- **Sig compile cost and enumeration on the new type** were neither measured nor ported. Since b092673, identify must stay under 250 method instances and 300 KB of native code per fresh mechanism.
- **The two-mode API** was never implemented: IndependentParameters, AllParameters, `dependent_parameters`, `all_parameters`, and the identity between the two modes. No report covers it, and the probe numbering skips P3.
- **Population coverage:**
  - Enumerated uni-uni has 1 mechanism and uni-bi 7.
  - The main data has no ter-ter. Skeptic P5a's 192 allo_terbi mechanisms feed only `:OnlyA` verdicts.
  - There was one expansion round over 60 parents, and only 500 of the 4,910 allo_bibi expansion children.
  - Inhibitor copies appear only in specs and handwritten.
  - Regulator sites appear in 22 allo_uniuni mechanisms and 6 specs.
  - There is no n = 3, and n = 4 appears only in specs.
- **Performance:**
  - Only 102 laws were timed, 12 of them allosteric and none ter-ter.
  - AllParameters laws were never timed.
  - In the past, a constructor-time solve made ter-ter enumeration 15× slower (#59).
- **Adversarial testing:** P4's c3 and Rule A and P8's key never saw an adversarial mechanism. P4 never defined a display for MWC laws.
- **P2's "40 of 40 enumerated count = rank"** was a sample; the full census found 5 exceptions.
- **The GC-drift fix is unmeasured.** Checked Int64 instead of `Rational{BigInt}` should remove that ROADMAP known issue, but only if the enumeration counter and the guard move off BigInt too.

## 4. Decisions for Denis

**D1. What `:OnlyA` means, and whether the package needs a limit engine at all.**
- **Evidence:**
  - With per-step classes, the limit equals deletion in 1,477/1,477 allo_bibi and 444/460 expand_bibi mechanisms.
  - The exceptions, and 80 of 81 enumerated `:OnlyA` ping-pong mechanisms, have no unique limit or sequester the enzyme.
  - Deletion is always thermodynamically consistent: P5b's deletion laws pass detailed balance to 1.1e-153.
  - But deletion admits tag sets whose law is not a limit of the active-state model: 32 grid sets, including the July trap, where the inactive state holds bound P that it can never release.
  - Today's guard errs both ways on mixed groups.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Deletion by definition, with no guard.
  - (b) Compute by deletion, admit by the limit:
    - an exact cone-feasibility test on per-step classes, which replaces today's guard and its constructor-time nullspace;
    - one named flip form (D7);
    - a structural rule standing in for G2/G3: reject an inactive-state island that only `:OnlyA` chemistry connects to the flip form.
  - (c) P5a's full limit engine.
- **Recommendation: (b).**
  - It keeps the limit's meaning wherever a limit exists, costs about 60–80 lines instead of about 250, and needs no leading-order engine.
  - The consequence is that the enumerator stops tagging both half-reaction chemistry steps of a ping-pong mechanism `:OnlyA`.
  - Pick (a) instead if you want those models and accept inactive states that hold bound species they can never release.

**D2. What kcat means.**
- **Evidence:**
  - The leading-order kcat matches `_kcat_forward` in 258/297 plain and 213/218 allosteric mechanisms. It is 0 in the 42 dead-end-capture cases and ∞ in the 4 that never saturate.
  - The ROADMAP's planned assay-concentration anchor deletes `_kcat_forward` (107 code lines), but it changes every reported rescaled constant.
  - Under D1(b), nothing else uses a leading-order engine.
- **Recommendation:** anchor kcat at assay concentrations. If an analytic kcat must stay, define it as the saturating limit with dead-end corners excluded, and raise an error when the mechanism never saturates.

**D3. RE cycles in AllParameters.**
- **Evidence:**
  - 27/806 plain mechanisms have an RE cycle that the kinetic grouping leaves open.
  - Without that cycle's relation, the RE subsystem has no equilibrium, and Full mode lists constants that never reach v.
  - In 23 of them, the law depends on breadth-first-search order.
- **Recommendation:** impose RE-only cycle relations in every mode. That adds 1–3 dependents in those 27 mechanisms, and it amends "Haldane/Wegscheider not applied".

**D4. Rational exponents.**
- **Evidence:** `half_keq` needs Keq^(−1/2) whichever constants are pivoted, and a priority that forced unit pivots would depend on the grouping.
- **Recommendation:** accept rational exponents in every stage. Codegen computes each `sqrt` once.

**D5. Grouped chemistry steps that change conformation in opposite directions.**
- **Evidence:** under P2's alignment the model changes (F1).
- **Recommendation:** tie forward with forward (today's Tier-1 rule), and take the alignment from the type layer, not the solve.

**D6. Flux-free steady-state steps (known issue 3).**
- **Evidence:**
  - At the group level, only hand-written mechanisms reach this: specs/14 and handwritten/17, and 0 of 751 enumerated mechanisms.
  - Converting such groups to rapid equilibrium makes the fitted count (the length of `fitted_params`) equal the identifiable rank in 4 of 5 known cases.
  - The conversion is exact for bridges in both modes, but for other flux-free steps only while Wegscheider relations are imposed.
  - The predicate is sound but partial: it never flags a step that carries flux, but it misses tie-induced zero flux and dead-end chains.
- **Recommendation:** reject such mechanisms at the DSL boundary with "write this step as ⇌". That is about 40 lines and needs no per-mode rules. It fits your rule that correctness bugs get fixed even when only hand-written mechanisms reach them, and the identifiable rank stays an analysis tool.

**D7. Two free forms and L.**
- **Evidence:**
  - No enumerated mechanism has two free forms.
  - Moving the flip within one RE segment, or across a dead-end isomerization, only rescales L.
  - Moving it across a ping-pong (37% mismatch) or across a flux-carrying isomerization (12–45%) changes the model.
  - Today's package flips at different forms in the two states (AD2e) and returns NaN on AD2c.
- **Recommendation:** one flip form per allosteric mechanism, chosen by content. Allow other free forms only inside its RE segment, and reject anything else. If you prefer flexibility, let the DSL declare the flip form instead.

## 5. Design implications the spec must encode

**Mechanism type and orientation**
1. Use one mechanism type for plain and allosteric mechanisms (P6):
   - forms sorted by content; edges as isbits values;
   - a group label per edge, a tag per group, sorted sites;
   - no reaction field; explicit `==` and `hash`;
   - inhibitors, regulators and metabolites derived, not stored.
2. Store edges by rule 1: a < b, with bindings stored taking their metabolite up. Derive physical-forward orientation with today's tiers, using tier 3 by form index. Naming, the solve priority, group alignment and the printer all read that orientation.
3. Align group members by that orientation, forward chemistry with forward (D5). The solve never re-derives alignment. The constructor requires each reaction to appear once and group members to take up and give off the same metabolites.
4. Split validation:
   - the constructor checks structure only;
   - the DSL and enumeration boundary checks bottomless segments, `:OnlyA` admission, atom conservation, colliding rendered names, and bindings that change a metabolite's role.
5. Encode the type parameter as one homogeneous integer tuple and decode it by splatting. Gate it with `test_compile_budget.jl`.
6. Represent the inactive state as an edge mask on the same step list, pruned from the one flip form. Names and orientation always come from the full mechanism.

**Engine**

7. Build one bare law per graph over per-step variables:
   - Cha contraction of RE segments;
   - whole-graph spanning trees oriented per root;
   - the edge-sum numerator;
   - no Hill numerator and no blocks.
8. Choose RE-segment roots and RE spanning trees by content. This keeps the polynomials independent of storage order and names, which today's root and P5b's first spanning tree violated.
9. Replace `_assert_derivable` with a cap on the spanning-tree count. Keep `_eq_complexity`, and make it count both conformations.

**Joint solve**

10. **Columns:** one per bare constant, plus an inactive-state copy for each non-`:EqualAI` group.
11. **Rows:**
    - the fundamental cycles of a spanning forest, with integer Haldane multiples;
    - kinetic-group equality rows;
    - `:EqualAI` ties;
    - RE-only cycle rows, in every mode.

    Inactive-state columns go before active-state columns.
12. **Priority:** the key (kind, occupied, ss, against), then the content name. The spec should state that this is invariant under storage order, direction, grouping and same-model regrouping, but not under renaming a metabolite.
13. Compute an exact RREF over checked Int64; on overflow, raise an error or retry in BigInt. Assert that 0 = log Keq cannot occur, given the alignment preconditions.
14. Dependents are power products with rational exponents, including Keq^(p/q). Pins to 1 and to Keq^c are dependents.
15. Fold every dependent whose right side is one symbol with exponent 1 into a single fold table that both conformations share.
16. Label each dependent `:kinetic_group`, `:haldane` (Keq on the right side) or `:wegscheider`. Do not promise a single Haldane dependent: 18/806 mechanisms have two. Name both rate-constant classes of a steady-state group from the same member.

**API and allosteric law**

17. **IndependentParameters:** the independent constants, Keq and E_total.
18. **AllParameters (plain mechanisms only):** one constant per group, minus the RE-cycle dependents.
19. Property test: `rate_equation(m, c, p) == rate_equation(m, c, all_parameters(m, p), AllParameters)` on every population.
20. Assemble the MWC law with P5b's cross-weighted formula. Drop factors equal to 1, cancel common monomials, and remove the "divide" branch. `:OnlyA` follows D1 and the flip form D7.

**Normalization, bridge and codegen**

21. **Generated code:** reduce to lowest terms by a concentration-monomial shift, and never divide by a polynomial.
22. **Printed strings:** use Rule A's reference (the constant term, else ΠS) and divide by its coefficient only.
23. Emit the IndependentParameters numerator as (ΠS − ΠP/Keq)·Q. Q is positive and may contain Keq (it does in 224/806 laws).
24. The bridge is a coefficient map plus an exact identity check by modular evaluation (mod 2^61−1 at random points). Never use naive rational-function arithmetic: it ran out of memory in P4.
25. **Kernel body:**
    - positional loads;
    - each dependent and each reciprocal computed once;
    - node locals for H, for each RE segment's σ and D, and for the MWC state, site and cross-weight terms;
    - greedy Horner with binary `+` and `*`;
    - integer powers written as products, and `sqrt` for half powers.
26. Derive inside the generator, with no global memo; derivation costs only tens of µs.
27. Extend the 0-allocation, sub-120-ns gate to the largest enumerated laws: expand_bibi/626, 642 and 658.

**Dedup key and process**

28. The key is SHA-256 of the canonical text of the IndependentParameters law (K1). Compute it before codegen. Store fits by canonical position, because today's copy-by-name breaks on 270 merges.
29. Pin `fitted_params` lengths in tests, and keep the identifiable rank an analysis tool.
30. Most of the correctness gain comes from P2 + P5b and does not need the engine rewrite: known issues 1 and 2, determinism, closed RE cycles. Horner codegen is independent and can ship first.
31. Expect test pins to move:

## Surprises and failures
- **The baseline unit trap.** The "~1,950 lines" baseline is physical lines. Comparing the probes' code-line estimates against it would show a false 50% cut.
- **The shared line counter undercounts.** `<proto>/p1_engine/codelines.sh` and `<proto>/p6/codelines.sh` undercount any range that starts inside a docstring: lines 561–752 of `rate_eq_derivation.jl` count 38 instead of 107. Whole-file counts are right, so P1's and P6's range counts may be low.
