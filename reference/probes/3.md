# Skeptic report: P1-engine

## Question

The P1 probe asked whether a fresh graph engine can be exactly correct and also simpler than today's derivation. Its verdict was PARTIAL: correct and about 2× faster, but not smaller. My job was to reproduce its numbers, then try to break each claim with mechanisms it did not cover.

The predicate fails on two shapes:
- **All turnover in one segment's self-loop, with SS leaves or chains attached** (A4 `E + I <--> E(I)`). The predicate only examines segments that sit in two or more blocks, so it never looks at these leaves.
- **A non-reactive bridge or block between two reactive parts** (A3: two active conformations joined by `E <--> F`).

Neither shape occurs in the 806 or in the 1,041 enumerated mechanisms; it takes hand-written structures.

### New failure cases

1. **A replacement predicate that is exact in every case I tried.** At consistent constants, an SS step is speed-free exactly when either holds:
   - it is a silent self-loop; or
   - its biconnected block contains no cycle with turnover.

   With independent constants, it is speed-free exactly when it is a silent self-loop or a bridge.

   This follows from flux decomposition: a step's net flux is the sum of the fluxes of the cycles through it; a cycle without turnover carries no flux under the Wegscheider relations; and in a biconnected block that has one reactive cycle, every edge lies on a reactive cycle. It matches the numeric tests in 806/806 (both regimes), in all 27 package-built and 5 direct mechanisms, and in all 1,041 enumerated ones.
2. **Polynomials depend on names.** One uni-uni with an RE isomerization ES ⇌ EP gives a different (num, den) when its product is named P (segment root EP) than when it is named T (root ES). The denominator terms differ by K^±1. A dedup key built on these polynomials is not rename-proof (`logs/rename_gauge.log`).
3. **Fractional exponents** (A10g), **the Hill limit** (A12, D5), **dfree is the root's weight** (A18, A18b, D1) and **non-identifiability beyond phantoms** are covered in the table above.

## Verdict

**PARTIAL.** I agree with the probe's verdict, but two of its design implications should not be relied on.

**The design can rely on the probe for the core:**
- the bare law is exactly the steady-state law;
- edge-sum and spanning-tree derivation are right;
- EngineLean equals the package everywhere both run, and also runs where Hill fails;
- the (1 − Γ/Keq) division works, with a positive quotient;
- RE cycles must be closed in every mode;
- the core is not meaningfully smaller than today's.

**It cannot rely on:**
- **Implication 6**, that the structural predicate is exact.
- **Implication 7**, that the allosteric normalization can be built on dfree.
- **Implication 1** as written: "monomial substitutions" need rational exponents.

## Design implications

1. Use the lean engine: whole-graph spanning trees, the edge-sum numerator and the (1 − Γ/Keq) division. Drop Hill (2^μ cost, hard limit at μ = 20) and block factorization.
2. The substitution layer and code generation must support fractional powers, or the pivot priority must avoid non-unit pivots.
3. Detect phantoms with either the block-level predicate or a numeric per-group scaling test on the Reduced law, and state which regime applies. Fold phantoms only in IndependentParameters mode.
4. If the spec promises identifiable fitted parameters, it needs a rank check. Phantoms are only 2 of the 36 lost dimensions.
5. Name the reference form explicitly and use weight = α_free·D[its segment], not the root's tree sum.
6. Choose segment roots without using names, or normalize the parameter gauge before building dedup keys or strings.
7. Keep `_eq_complexity`. A tree-count cap replaces only `_assert_derivable`.

## Open questions for Denis

1. **Identifiability:** should fitted_params be guaranteed identifiable (rank equals count), or is removing speed-free phantoms enough? Most lost dimensions come from the iso gauge and textbook microscopic constants.
2. **Fractional exponents:** should they be allowed (sqrt in generated code), or should the pivot priority prefer unit coefficients? The second makes the priority depend on group membership.
3. **Which free form carries L** when there are several: E and F in different segments (A3), or E and D in one segment (A18)? This is the open ROADMAP question; today the answer falls out of species order and names.

That column is A5's `k_E_Iinh_to_EIinh`, whose reverse constant is its dependent. I fixed it by zeroing any column below 1e-40 of the largest, and validated the fix on four known cases:

Patched copies run and reproduce the probe's numbers.
