## Question
Can a stdlib-only data layer and numeric oracle be built that the other probes can trust? Concretely:
- export the package's mechanisms and outputs as plain data;
- solve the full mass-action network in BigFloat;
- show the oracle agrees with thermodynamics and with the package to 1e-30 and 1e-12.

**Why the 17 disagree.** An exact structural test (`Pop.re_cycle_closure`) marks an RE cycle as "open" when the kinetic grouping does not make its Wegscheider relation hold automatically. Every disagreement has an open RE cycle, and every agreement has none.

The cause is in `_compute_alpha`: it builds the rapid-equilibrium weights along a breadth-first spanning tree. The K of the edge that closes the cycle therefore never enters the rate law. In 21 of 21 such expand_bibi mechanisms, a parameter listed by `parameters(em, Full)` is absent from the Full polynomial.

**Other findings at HEAD ffb36ad:**
- **The Pass-2 fold changes the Full-mode polynomials in 3 of 806 plain mechanisms.** All three are specs: Non-competitive Inhibitor, Non-essential Activator, and Activator + Competitive Inhibitor. Full mode ties `K_ERinhS_to_ERinh_S` to `K_ES_to_E_S`, so the absorbed constant has no effect. The fold never fires on the 751 enumerated plain mechanisms.
- **ROADMAP known issue 1 reproduces:** fitted names outside the declared constants in 98 of 1477 allo_bibi mechanisms and 17 of 460 allosteric expand_bibi mechanisms. In allo_bibi/1, for example, the inactive state renames an `:EqualAI` group `K_EBQ_to_EQ_B` instead of `K_EAB_to_EA_B`.
- **Known issue 2 reproduces:** 4 mechanisms fold the same pair in opposite directions in the two states, and 30 fold in exactly one state.
- **A new phantom in spec "Numerator: redundant SS-bind".** It fits 6 parameters, but only 5 are identifiable. The SS step `E + S1 <--> ES1` lies inside one RE segment; both of its constants are fitted, yet neither appears in the rate polynomials, only their ratio through the dependents. No enumerated mechanism has this (0 of 751).
- **Performance baseline.**
  - Per-call `eval_ns`: median 2–7 ns per population.
  - Three all-SS mechanisms (expand_bibi/626, 642, 658; `eq_complexity` 672, 396 denominator terms) take 115.6–122.3 ns. They are not covered by the 120 ns gate.
  - Correlation of `eq_complexity` with `eval_ns`: 0.92.
  - Constructor median: 13.8 µs plain, 56 µs allosteric.
  - First `rate_equation` call (derivation plus compile): median 7 ms, max 0.26 s.

## Verdict
**PASS.** The data covers every requested population and records every stage error. The oracle meets both criteria: detailed balance to 1e-107 and Full-mode agreement to 7e-15 on every mechanism where Full mode is well-defined.

The only gap is where Full mode itself is not a well-defined rate law: an RE cycle the grouping leaves open, or a Pass-2 fold. That gap is now an exact structural predicate rather than a tolerance.

Checks (iii) and (iv) show the current plain derivation is numerically correct on all 255 mechanisms, so it is a valid reference for the redesign. Allosteric mechanisms have no independent oracle here.

## Design implications
1. **AllParameters needs a stated rule for RE cycles.** A rapid-equilibrium subsystem only has an equilibrium if its cycles satisfy their relations, so "Haldane/Wegscheider not applied" cannot cover them. Otherwise AllParameters lists a constant that never reaches v: 21 of 240 plain expand_bibi children, 3 specs and 3 handwritten mechanisms.
2. **Several constants enter only as a ratio:**
   - the K that closes an open RE cycle;
   - an SS step inside one RE segment whose cycle runs no reaction (the redundant SS-bind spec);
   - the dead-end bridge (ROADMAP known issue 3).

   All three can be read from the graph. Each is either a dependent or one equilibrium constant, never two fitted constants.
3. **Baselines for the redesign** (current value → target):

   | Metric | Today | Target |
   |---|---|---|
   | Phantom fitted names | 115 | 0 |
   | Opposite-direction folds | 4 | 0 |
   | Folds in one state only | 30 | 0 |
   | kcat failures | 4 | 0 |
   | Fitted vs identifiable count | spec 14 over by 1 | equal |
   | Enumerated mechanisms over 120 ns | 1 (2 more above 115 ns) | 0 |

4. **The oracle runs fast.** Validating 255 mechanisms takes 2.5 s, so other probes can afford it on whole populations.

## Surprises and failures
- The dependent strings cannot be parsed back when names carry residuals. I added monomials and re-exported everything once.
- `pkill -f` matched my own shell and killed it; no harm done.
- The lock was held twice by another agent's `Pkg.test`, about 6 minutes each.
- The populations are smaller than budgeted: expand_bibi 700 (cap 2000), allo_uniuni 35, init_uniuni 1.
- Zero derivation failures on allo_bibi, although the prototype's aggregate test still guards against bottomless rapid-equilibrium segments.
- The task's Full-mode criterion did not foresee RE cycles; the 17 disagreements are a property of Full mode, not of the oracle.
- No Julia process is left running.
