# Skeptic report: probe P2-solve (joint log-linear solve)

## Question

Does P2's evidence hold up? Specifically: (1) is the joint RREF over bare per-step constants, with one content-only column priority, deterministic and correct? (2) Do the flux-free extension and the solve fix the parameter-count phantoms? I re-ran every P2 script, rebuilt the key checks with methods independent of `Thermo.jl`, and attacked the solve with adversarial mechanisms (package-built and hand-built) plus 1,200 random mechanisms.

**New failure cases.** Logs: `adversarial.log`, `followup.log`, `flux_census.log`, `last_checks.log`.

- **F1. Group alignment for isomerisation members differs from the package.**
  - The case: `(E(S) <--> F(P), F(S) <--> E(P))`, and `(F(S) <--> E(P), E(I) <--> F(I))`.
  - P2's `group_alignment` (Thermo.jl:271-291) aligns members that take up nothing by their conformational change. So it ties forward chemistry to reverse chemistry.
  - The package keeps the Tier-1 (chemistry) orientation: `_orient_tied_steps` re-orients only steps that Tier 1 leaves tied.
  - Counts agree (9 and 8), but the models differ:
    - package constants rebuilt from P2's independents are off by 6.2e3 and 1.6e2;
    - v differs by 5–57% and 1–101%;
    - in the opposite-chemistry case the chemistry drops out of P2's Haldane relation, leaving a binding off-rate `= Keq^-2·…`.
  - P2's census check ("aligned-vs-stored: 0 of 938 groups") cannot see this, because no population group has this form.
- **F2. Validation gaps.**
  - P2 accepts `(E <--> F, F <--> G)`, which the package rejects, and returns `k:G()->E() = k:F()->E()^2 · …`.
  - On parallel identical steps (also package-rejected), it returns two dependents with the same name, `k:E(P) -> E(S)`.
- **F3. Rational exponents.** Fractional exponents, including `Keq^(1/2)`, occur on package-valid mechanisms (F1, `half_keq`, `half_exponent`).
- **F4. Missed zero-flux steps.** Steps whose zero flux follows from ties plus a thermodynamic pin are not flagged.
  - `tc_pair_iso` (`(E+S <--> F+P, F+S <--> E+P); F <--> E; E+S ⇌ F(S)`): package and P2 count 3, FD rank 2, and E <--> F has flux exactly 0.
  - The same happens in `symmetric_routes_pinned` (ES <--> FS) and fuzz/311.
- **F5. The conversion holds only when thermodynamics is imposed.**
  - With independent per-class constants (AllParameters semantics), v(RE-converted) vs v(SS) differ by 0.9 (`exchange_in_wegscheider_cycle`), 0.8 (`ss_in_re_segment`), 3.0 (`inh_copy_iso_cycle`) and 0.1 (`dead_end_ss_triangle`).
  - For plain bridges they agree to 1e-111.
  - Every non-bridge conversion also opens an RE cycle in AllParameters.
- **F6. Dead-end branches of two or more forms keep a phantom after conversion.**
  - `hand:re_dead_end_chain` (RE uni-uni + `E + I ⇌ E(I) ⇌ F(I)`): count 5, rank 4.
  - `dead_end_ss_chain` and `dead_end_ss_triangle`: count after conversion 7, rank 4 (2 of the gap are the SS uni-uni's).
  - Of 150 random mechanisms that were rank-checked, 104 still have count > rank after conversion.
- **F7. Naming wrinkle.**
  - An SS group's two constant classes can take their representatives from different members.
  - Example `hand:crossed_reps`: the on-rate is named after `E(J)+S <--> F(J,S)` and the off-rate after `F(I)+S <--> E(I,S)`.

## Verdict

**PARTIAL.**

**What holds.** The core solve is deterministic and defines the correct constraint set wherever group semantics match the package:
- 806 population mechanisms, 20 of 22 package-built adversarial mechanisms, and 1,200 random mechanisms;
- 0 detailed-balance failures, 0 contradictions and 0 overflows.

**What needs three spec fixes:** group alignment (F1, F2), validation preconditions (F2), and rational exponents (F3).

**What does not hold as stated.** The "fixes the phantoms" half of P2's PASS:
- the flux-free rule fixes the narrow ROADMAP-3 class (single dead-end steps) only;
- it misses tie-induced zero flux (F4);
- its conversion is valid only under thermodynamics (F5);
- phantoms remain in dead-end chains (F6) and in 5 enumerated ping-pong mechanisms that P2's 40-mechanism sample missed.

Allosteric known issues 1 and 2 are UNVERIFIABLE here: no A/I tie rows were built.

## Design implications

1. Orient group members with the constructor's rule: Tier-1 progress first, then conformation, rejecting mismatched conformational changes. Do not re-derive alignment inside the solve. This also fixes the free-isomerisation "forward" pick (`tc_iso_uni_uni`), since that needs Tier 2.
2. State the preconditions the content-only naming relies on: each reaction appears in one step, and group members share their metabolites and their conformational change.
3. Dependents carry rational exponents, including on Keq. Codegen and every symbolic stage (dedup key, normalization, kcat, limits) must accept half-integers. Forcing unit pivots would make the priority depend on grouping.
4. Keep flux-free detection as a sound, structural, partial rule. Phantom counting should not rest on it: monomial collisions in RE segments and tie-induced zero flux need the rank, or a broader rule.
5. Any conversion of flux-free steps must be specified per mode. It is exact for plain bridges, but only valid with Wegscheider imposed for other flux-free steps.
6. Name a group's two SS classes consistently, for example by the representative of the forward class.

## Open questions for Denis

1. **Grouped chemistry steps that change conformation in opposite directions.** Tie forward to forward, as the package does today, or by conformational change, as P2 does? I recommend forward to forward.
2. **Flux-free conversion.** Options:
   - (a) convert plain bridges only (exact in both modes);
   - (b) convert all flux-free steps in IndependentParameters only, and have `all_parameters` fill the phantom rate magnitude by convention;
   - (c) do not convert, and report phantoms through the rank.
3. **Exponents.** Accept rational exponents (my recommendation), or constrain the priority to unit pivots at the cost of making it depend on grouping?

## Surprises and failures

- **My mistake.** My first fuzz run stored steps in random directions while my reference applied package ties to the stored orientation. That produced 9 false tie failures and 2 false contradictions. The fixed run, with canonical storage, has 0 of each. I keep both runs (`fuzz.log` vs `fuzz_flip.log`): the false contradictions show that the metabolite-alignment precondition in P2's "0 = log Keq cannot occur" argument is necessary.
- **Phantom spread.** Phantoms in enumerated mechanisms come from monomial collisions inside one RE segment (E(Q) and E(B; res)), not from dead ends. The package's own test fixture handwritten/23 has the same phantom.
- **The package agrees on fractional exponents.** It also emits `Keq^-0.5` (`half_keq`, `iso_group_mixed_tier`), so this is not specific to P2.
