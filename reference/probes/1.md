## Question
Can a fresh graph engine be both exactly correct and simpler than today's? The engine as specified uses:
- one symbol per step constant;
- Cha contraction of rapid-equilibrium (RE) segments;
- undirected spanning trees per biconnected block, oriented per root;
- a Hill cycle-flux numerator.

**Structure findings**
- **177 mechanisms have a step constant absent from the bare law.** In 176 it is the K that closes an RE cycle; in 1 it is a silent SS self-loop (specs/14). The structural prediction is exact.
- **The RE spanning-tree (BFS) order changes the Full-mode law in 23 mechanisms** (expand_bibi/3, /43, /120 and 18 more; handwritten/12, /15). It never changes the Reduced law (0/806).
- Substitution never moved the lowest-terms shift (0/806).
- **Speed-free SS steps** (the law is unchanged when kf and kr scale together): 194 steps in 175 mechanisms. The structural predicate matches the numeric test in 806/806 and in the synthetic cases. It covers dead-end branches at a cut segment with no turnover cycle, and SS self-loops with ω = 0.
- **At the kinetic-group level only 2 are phantoms in the package**, confirmed on its own reduced polynomials:
  - specs/14, step `E + S1 <--> ES1`;
  - handwritten/17, step `EQ + A::Inh <--> EAinhQ`.
- **None of the 751 enumerated mechanisms has one,** because each of their 192 speed-free steps shares a group with a productive step.

## Verdict
**PARTIAL.**
- **Exactly correct: pass.** All 806 match exactly, the oracle and synthetic cases agree, and there is no mismatch to explain.
- **Faster: yes,** about 2× (full) and 2.7× (lean).
- **Clearly smaller: fail.**
  - The requested design is 411 code lines (Engine + Poly) against today's 242. I estimate about 365 in production.
  - The lean variant is 227 lines (about 205 in production) and adds the Haldane division. That is roughly equal to today's, not clearly smaller.
  - Code reduction will have to come from the thermodynamics, Pass-2 and allosteric code, not from this core.

## Design implications
1. **Derive one bare law per graph on integer per-step variables.** Kinetic groups, folds, dependents and allosteric state labels are all monomial substitutions applied afterwards. Normalize to lowest terms last. This reproduces the package exactly in both raw and Reduced form.
2. **Compute tree sums from undirected spanning trees oriented per root, replacing `sym_det`.** The size guard becomes a tree-count cap inside the enumeration, about 2 lines.
3. **Use the edge-sum numerator** (6 lines) and, in IndependentParameters, write it as (1 − Γ/Keq)·Q by exact chain division (about 20 lines). Q has only positive terms and half the size, so generated numerator code halves. Hill's cycle flux (78 lines) produces the identical polynomial and is not needed for derivation.
4. **Leave block factorization out of v1** (74 lines). After group renaming it saves 2.6% of terms and nothing on the largest laws.
5. **The spec must impose RE-cycle relations in every mode.** Otherwise the law depends on which spanning tree the BFS picks: 23 Full-mode laws here. This is the Foundation agent's question 1.
6. **A kinetic group is a phantom exactly when every step in it is speed-free.** The structural predicate is exact; a per-group scaling test on the reduced law would also work.
7. dfree matches the package 806/806, so the allosteric normalization can be built on it.

## Open questions for Denis
1. Should the numerator be the edge sum plus exact (1 − Γ/Keq) division instead of Hill's cycle flux? Same polynomial, 78 fewer lines. I recommend yes.
2. Should block factorization be dropped from the derivation? I recommend yes.
3. Should a speed-free kinetic group be fitted as one equilibrium constant instead of kf and kr? This changes `fitted_params` for "Numerator: redundant SS-bind" (6 → 5) and handwritten/17. I recommend yes.

## Surprises and failures
- The requested design costs more code than today's, so I built the lean variant to measure the alternative instead of estimating it.
- At the step level, 175 mechanisms looked like a large phantom class. At the group level it is 2, because enumeration groups each dead-end step with a productive one.
- Kinetic grouping erases most of the factoring gain: bare −21%, named −2.6%.
- Exact agreement on mechanisms with open RE cycles depends on copying the package's BFS scan order.
- A constructor method ambiguity in Poly (`P{T}(::Mono)`) is fixed with `term`.
- Timing is noisy under load: the same mechanism took 1.46 ms in one run and 2.05 ms in the other, but the median ratio held at 0.54–0.55 in both.
- No Julia process is left running.
