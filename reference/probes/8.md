## Question
Can both conformations come from one step list, with one joint solve, and remove the open allosteric parameter-count bugs without breaking anything else?
- The inactive (I) constants are relabeled copies of the bare constants, and `:EqualAI` groups share the active (A) column.
- Names come from the whole mechanism.
- The joint solve takes A cycles, I cycles, kinetic-group rows and `:EqualAI` tie rows, and eliminates I columns before A columns.
- The open bugs are ROADMAP known issue 1 (second names) and known issue 2 (folds).
- `:OnlyA` keeps today's semantics: delete the step, then keep only forms reachable from free enzyme.

## Verdict
**PASS.**
- (a), (b), (e) and (f) hold on all 1,993 mechanisms.
- Every count difference (149) is a known-issue case, and in every one my law is the package's restricted to the declared model.
- Same-model values agree to the package's Float64 precision.
- An independent oracle confirms the n = 1 assembly.

Caveats:
- `:OnlyA` is still deletion plus pruning.
- There is no independent oracle for n > 1.
- Names come from the package's whole-mechanism rendering (`step_names_A`/`_I`); I did not re-derive them from content.

## Design implications
1. **The inactive state is the same step list.** Never rebuild or re-canonicalize it; take names and orientation from the full mechanism. This alone removes all 142 second names.
2. **One joint solve with one fold table, applied to both states.** Rows are A cycles, I cycles, group rows per state and `:EqualAI` ties; I columns come first. This removes all 39 fold hazards. Collapses (K_I = K_A, an SS affinity collapsing while its speed stays free) come out of the solve with no special code. Int64 has ample room.
3. **The named law needs a content-chosen RE spanning tree**, or dependent RE constants on RE cycles written through independents (the 3-line fix used here). Otherwise the generated code and the dedup key depend on storage order.
4. **One normalization rule:** `v = (d_I^n·N_A·Q_A^(n-1)·W_A + L·d_A^n·N_I·Q_I^(n-1)·W_I) / (d_I^n·Q_A^n·W_A + L·d_A^n·Q_I^n·W_I)`. Drop factors equal to 1, and cancel common monomial factors. Delete the "divide" branch (0 of 1,993). The cross form must stay: 16 ping-pong mechanisms need it.
5. **Printing:** order terms by tag-free role keys, treat every non-concentration symbol as a parameter, and omit factors equal to 1.
6. **L carrier rule:** when two free forms are joined by a metabolite-free path, it is a reparametrization; use the existing segment-root rule. When the path exchanges metabolites, reject the mechanism and ask for a residual on the covalent form.
7. **Test pins move:** fitted lists change in 398 of the 1,844 same-model mechanisms, and in 4 of the 10 collapse fixtures. Counts move only in the known-issue mechanisms.

## Surprises and failures
- **The textbook MWC Dimer spec (RE, n = 2) has rank 6 for 7 parameters, in both laws.** The denominator only sees the first two moments of a two-point distribution in (1/K_S, 1/K_P) with weights 1/(1+L) and L/(1+L), which leaves one free direction. Holding L fixed restores full rank.
- My first RE fix substituted every dependent RE constant and expanded laws that have no RE cycle. I narrowed it to RE-cycle steps; determinism held.
- My tag-stripping symmetry check briefly reported 2 false mismatches (handwritten/27). The I law mixed `K_A_EP` and `K_I_EP`, which the stripping merged.
- I fixed redundant parentheses in single-factor branches.
- 4 allo_bibi mechanisms carry a phantom name at an equal count; these are pure misnamings.
- I waited about 2 minutes once on the lock for another agent's run. No Julia process is left running.
